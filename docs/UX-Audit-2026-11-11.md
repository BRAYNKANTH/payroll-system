Product: World Entrepreneurs Company payroll and hub accounts (local demo build at commit 76ccc57). Date: 2026-11-11.

## What is already strong

- Lighthouse accessibility 100 on sign-in and dashboard; 0 unnamed controls and exactly one h1 on all 15 pages.
- Phone navigation is a labelled 5-item bottom bar with aria-current; skip link, focus outlines and reduced motion are in place.
- CLS 0, TBT 50ms, no third-party scripts, fonts self-hosted; desktop dashboard Lighthouse performance 99.
- Forms: visible labels, error summaries that link to fields, step-by-step phone flows, loading states on buttons.
- Safe-area padding, 16px gutters and cards instead of wide tables on 12 lists.

## Task walkthroughs

Evidence for these comes from today's measurements and from the phone testing done earlier in this project. I did not re-run each flow end to end today.

1. **Hub manager enters the daily sheet on a phone.** Home, Daily (1 tap). "One by one" shows one person with large fields and Save and next. Friction: the Grid view is 22px-high inputs on Weekly and Stock (finding 1). Drop-off risk: medium on Weekly, low on Daily.
2. **Accountant runs the month.** Payroll run (5.8 screens, but the current step is pinned at the top), then Approvals (7.1 screens), then finalize, then Payslips. Friction: Approvals length (finding 9); Payroll sideways scroll at 320px (finding 4). Drop-off risk: medium.
3. **Distributor opens a payslip in the portal.** My page with bottom tabs, payslip available once the month is finalized. Friction: English only (finding 3). Not re-measured today.

## Quick wins (high impact, under a day)

Findings 1, 2, 4, 7, 13 and 14 are each a CSS or JSON line or two. Doing all six would clear every High finding except the Tamil one, and the sideways scrolling.

## What I could not verify

- Screen reader behaviour (VoiceOver, TalkBack): needs a recording on a real phone (checkpoints 118, 117).
- On-screen keyboard overlap and sticky bars (7, 84): needs a real phone.
- Field performance (125, 126): needs a real-user report from the hosted site; today's numbers are lab simulations on the local demo.
- Widths 360, 390, 414, 768, landscape, foldables, 200% and 400% zoom (3, 5, 14, 55, 56).
- Hosted compression and first-request wake-up (128, 136): measured only locally.
- Colour-blindness, outdoor readability, OS large text (67, 68, 124).
- The staff portal, which was not re-measured today.
